What is a karaoke izakaya?

An izakaya is a Japanese pub where people share small dishes over drinks. A karaoke izakaya adds a microphone: you sing right at your table, in front of everyone, between bites. No private booths — just the whole room cheering you on. It's a very Japanese way to spend an evening, and you'll rarely find it anywhere else.

1. Sit down

Take a seat at the counter or a table and order a drink.

2. Eat & drink

Try mama's home cooking — the baked tomato & cheese is a favorite.

3. Sing!

Pick a song and take the mic — ¥200 per song.

5 reasons to drop by

Karaoke for ¥200 a song

02

Great value

The table charge is just ¥500. Chuhai ¥460, snacks from ¥500. You can sing and drink without the high prices of a snack bar — friendly enough to drop by every night after work.

03

A kind master (Momo-chan) & mama

The master, Momo-chan, and "mama" greet first-timers and solo travelers with a warm smile.

04

Delicious home cooking

The place used to be a teppanyaki restaurant, so griddle dishes are the stars. Dashimaki rolled omelette, negima and baked tomato & cheese are big hits with the regulars — and perfect with a drink.

Friendly regulars

Sit next to someone and you'll soon be chatting — and singing — together.

  • Buy 2 booksget 2 free tickets

How it works

  1. 1Hand over one ticket before you sing. One ticket per song; a medley takes 3.
  2. 2Add your next song only after the current one has finished.
  3. 3Scoring game: score a number ending in today's date, or that number +5, and win a prize. One point short? Try the lottery for a ¥100 donation.
  4. 4Don't want a score? Just say 「点数なしで」 (tensū nashi de) before you sing. That's ¥200 per song.
  5. 5We can keep up to 2 ticket books for you (bottle-keep guests only). Please take the rest home.
